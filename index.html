About me

Researcher in electrical, electronic and automation engineering.

Writing my engineering thesis at the Salamanca thermal power plant and interning at the RIAMA refinery confirmed my passion for control and digital systems.

Curiosity led me to a master’s degree in electrical engineering, specialising in digital systems and control. A sense of adventure helped me reset my life and move to Spain, where I earned a PhD in Electrical, Electronic and Automation Engineering.

I enjoy learning something new every day, and running even more. My greatest achievement was finishing my first marathon. My greatest pleasure: eating taquitos and mole.
